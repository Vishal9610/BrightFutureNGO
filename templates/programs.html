Our Programs

We work to improve the lives of street children through education, support, and essential learning resources.

Free Education

We provide free education to street children through learning centers and community classes to help them build a better future.

Book Distribution

We distribute books, notebooks and study materials so children can continue their education without financial difficulties.

School Bag Distribution

Every year we provide school bags and educational kits to children from poor families.
